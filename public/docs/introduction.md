# Introduction to Sekersoft

Sekersoft is a technology company dedicated to digitalizing business operations through efficient, secure, and user-friendly software. We bridge the gap between complex enterprise software and agile business needs by providing solutions that are easy to adopt and locally supported.

## What We Do
We develop high-performance applications for Web, Mobile, and Desktop environments. Our primary focus is on helping businesses transition from manual, paper-based workflows to streamlined digital processes. Our flagship product, Sekersoft Lojistik, is a testament to our commitment to solving real-world operational challenges.

## Target Audience
- **Small to Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs)**: Looking to professionalize their operations.
- **Logistics & Transportation Companies**: Seeking better cost control and order management.
- **Business Owners**: Who value data security (offline-first) and direct, local support.
- **Operation Managers**: Who need measurable results and real-time reporting.
