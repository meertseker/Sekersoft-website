# Sekersoft Products & Services

## Product: Sekersoft Lojistik

### Description
A professional offline-first desktop management system designed specifically for transportation and logistics businesses. It allows companies to manage orders, routes, and costs from a single, secure dashboard.

### Use Cases
- **Small Fleet Owners**: To track individual vehicle profitability and hidden costs.
- **Logistics Companies**: For digitalizing order workflows and generating end-of-month reports.
- **Private Transport Services**: To manage recurring routes and customer-specific pricing.

### Input
- **Order Details**: Customer info, vehicle plate, route (start/end), price.
- **Cost Data**: Fuel prices, driver wages, maintenance expenses, highway/bridge tolls.
- **Documents**: PDF or image uploads for invoices and logistics paperwork.

### Output
- **Automated Cost Calculation**: Instant visibility into the profitability of every order.
- **Financial Reports**: Monthly summaries of income, expenses, and net profit.
- **Performance Analytics**: Visual charts showing top-performing vehicles and customers.
- **Exports**: CSV and JSON exports for accounting and further analysis.
